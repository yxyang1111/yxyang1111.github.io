I am Yuxin Yang, a Ph.D. student in Computer Architecture at the School of Computer Science, Peking University. I work with Guojie Luo in the DASYS Lab, Center for Energy-Efficient Computing and Applications (CECA).

My research focuses on efficient large language model systems and AI compilers, spanning inference and training infrastructure, heterogeneous computing, and hardware–software co-design. I received my bachelor’s degree from Peking University in 2023.
